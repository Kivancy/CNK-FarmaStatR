# Popülasyon biyoeşdeğerliği (PBE): FDA PSG 020929

## 1. Soru ve kapsam
Test (T) ve referans (R) ürününün in vitro ölçümleri (ör. verilen doz, nebülizasyon süresi, impaktör boyutlu kütle) ortalama ve toplam değişkenlik bakımından eşdeğer mi? PBE, T ile R arasındaki farkı R’nin kendi içindeki farkla karşılaştırır. Yöntem FDA’nın budesonid inhalasyon süspansiyonu ürün kılavuzunun (PSG 020929, Aralık 2025) Ek A’sındaki beş adımdır; platform bu adımları değiştirilmemiş validasyonlu çekirdekle (`calc_pbe`) uygular.

## 2. Model ve gösterim
Her ürün için ℓ seri, seri başına n birim (kap, ampul, cihaz) ve birim başına m yaşam evresi (ör. başlangıç, orta, son) ölçülür. $X_{ijks}$: k ürününde (T ya da R) j serisindeki i biriminin s evresindeki ölçümü. Hesap log ölçeğindedir; ölçümleriniz ham ölçekteyse ekranda log dönüşümünü işaretleyin.

- $\mu_T, \mu_R$: ürün ortalamaları (log ölçeği); $\sigma_T^2, \sigma_R^2$: toplam varyanslar.
- $\sigma_{T0} = 0{,}1$ ve $\theta_p$: düzenleyici sabitler.
- MSB: birimler arası kareler ortalaması; MSW: birim içi (evreler arası) kareler ortalaması.
- Seriler havuzlanır: üç seri, seri varyansını güvenilir tahmin etmeye yetmediğinden toplam varyans “süper seri”nin birimler arası varyansı olarak tahmin edilir (FDA 1999, 18 Ağustos istatistik bilgisi).

## 3. Denklemler
Kriter (Adım 1). Hangisinin kullanılacağına tahmin edilen $\sigma_R$ karar verir:

$$ \eta_1 = (\mu_T-\mu_R)^2 + (\sigma_T^2-\sigma_R^2) - \theta_p\,\sigma_R^2 \quad (\sigma_R > \sigma_{T0}) $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 1
$$ \eta_2 = (\mu_T-\mu_R)^2 + (\sigma_T^2-\sigma_R^2) - \theta_p\,\sigma_{T0}^2 \quad (\sigma_R \le \sigma_{T0}) $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 1
$$ \theta_p = \frac{(\ln 1{,}11)^2 + 0{,}01}{0{,}1^2} = 2{,}0891 $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 1

Kareler ortalamaları (Adım 2); $\bar X_{ijk\cdot}$ birimin m evre ortalaması, $\bar X_{\cdot\cdot k\cdot}$ ürün ortalaması:

$$ MSB_k = \frac{m \sum_{j=1}^{\ell_k}\sum_{i=1}^{n_k} (\bar X_{ijk\cdot} - \bar X_{\cdot\cdot k\cdot})^2}{n_k \ell_k - 1} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 2
$$ MSW_k = \frac{\sum_{j}\sum_{i}\sum_{s=1}^{m} (X_{ijks} - \bar X_{ijk\cdot})^2}{n_k \ell_k (m-1)} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 2
$$ \hat\sigma_k = \sqrt{\frac{MSB_k}{m} + \frac{(m-1)\,MSW_k}{m}}, \qquad \hat\Delta = \bar X_{\cdot\cdot T\cdot} - \bar X_{\cdot\cdot R\cdot} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 1 ve 3

Doğrusallaştırılmış nokta tahmini ve %95 üst güven sınırı (Adım 4; α = 0,05). Her bileşen için $E_q$ nokta tahmini, $H_q$ güven sınırı ve $U_q = (H_q - E_q)^2$:

$$ E_D = \hat\Delta^2, \qquad H_D = \left(|\hat\Delta| + t_{1-\alpha,\; n_T\ell_T + n_R\ell_R - 2}\sqrt{\frac{MSB_T}{n_T\ell_T m} + \frac{MSB_R}{n_R\ell_R m}}\right)^2 $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 4
$$ E_1 = \frac{MSB_T}{m}, \qquad H_1 = \frac{(\ell_T n_T - 1)\,E_1}{\chi^2_{\ell_T n_T - 1,\;\alpha}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 4
$$ E_2 = \frac{(m-1)\,MSW_T}{m}, \qquad H_2 = \frac{\ell_T n_T (m-1)\,E_2}{\chi^2_{\ell_T n_T (m-1),\;\alpha}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 4
$$ E_{3s} = -(1+\theta_p)\frac{MSB_R}{m}, \qquad H_{3s} = \frac{(\ell_R n_R - 1)\,E_{3s}}{\chi^2_{\ell_R n_R - 1,\;1-\alpha}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 4
$$ E_{4s} = -(1+\theta_p)\frac{(m-1)\,MSW_R}{m}, \qquad H_{4s} = \frac{\ell_R n_R (m-1)\,E_{4s}}{\chi^2_{\ell_R n_R (m-1),\;1-\alpha}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 4
$$ H_{\eta 1} = (E_D + E_1 + E_2 + E_{3s} + E_{4s}) + \sqrt{U_D + U_1 + U_2 + U_{3s} + U_{4s}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 4; Hyslop ve ark. (2000) §3
$$ H_{\eta 2} = (E_D + E_1 + E_2 + E_{3c} + E_{4c} - \theta_p\sigma_{T0}^2) + \sqrt{U_D + U_1 + U_2 + U_{3c} + U_{4c}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 4

Sabit ölçekli kriterde $E_{3c} = -MSB_R/m$ ve $E_{4c} = -(m-1)MSW_R/m$ (H ve U terimleri aynı serbestlik dereceleriyle). $\chi^2_{\nu,\alpha}$, ν serbestlik dereceli ki-kare dağılımının α kantilidir: $\Pr(\chi^2_\nu \le \chi^2_{\nu,\alpha}) = \alpha$. m = 1 ise $E_2$, $E_4$ ve bunlara ait H, U terimleri hesaba girmez. Üst sınırın yapısı Howe (1974) yaklaşımının Hyslop ve ark. (2000) tarafından genişletilmesidir.

Değiştirilmiş tek yönlü PBE (yalnız ürün kılavuzu öngörüyorsa). Test ortalaması referanstan küçükse ortalama farkı kritere girmez:

$$ \frac{(\sigma_T^2-\sigma_R^2)}{\sigma_R^2} \le \theta_p \quad (\mu_T < \mu_R): \qquad E_D = H_D = U_D = 0 $$ :: FDA PSG 020121 (flutikazon propiyonat nazal sprey, Aralık 2025) Ek, Adım 1–3

## 4. Karar kuralı
$$ H_\eta \le 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{PBE sağlanır} $$ :: PSG 020929 Ek A, Adım 5; FDA (2026) Statistical Approaches to Establishing Bioequivalence

$H_\eta$, tahmin edilen $\sigma_R > 0{,}1$ ise referans ölçekli ($H_{\eta 1}$), değilse sabit ölçekli ($H_{\eta 2}$) sınırdır (Adım 3).

# Population bioequivalence (PBE): FDA PSG 020929

## 1. Question and scope
Are the in vitro measurements (e.g. delivered dose, nebulization time, impactor-sized mass) of the test (T) and reference (R) products equivalent in mean and in total variability? PBE compares the difference between T and R with the difference of R with itself. The method is the five steps in Appendix A of FDA's product-specific guidance for budesonide inhalation suspension (PSG 020929, December 2025); the platform applies them with the unchanged validated core (`calc_pbe`).

## 2. Model and notation
For each product, ℓ batches, n units (containers, ampoules, devices) per batch and m life stages per unit (e.g. beginning, middle, end) are measured. $X_{ijks}$: measurement of unit i of batch j of product k (T or R) at stage s. The computation is on the log scale; if your measurements are on the raw scale, tick the log transform on the screen.

- $\mu_T, \mu_R$: product means (log scale); $\sigma_T^2, \sigma_R^2$: total variances.
- $\sigma_{T0} = 0.1$ and $\theta_p$: regulatory constants.
- MSB: between-unit mean square; MSW: within-unit (between-stage) mean square.
- Batches are pooled: three batches are not enough to estimate the batch variance reliably, so the total variance is estimated as the between-unit variance of the “super-batch” (FDA 1999, statistical information of 18 August).

## 3. Equations
Criterion (Step 1). The estimated $\sigma_R$ decides which one applies:

$$ \eta_1 = (\mu_T-\mu_R)^2 + (\sigma_T^2-\sigma_R^2) - \theta_p\,\sigma_R^2 \quad (\sigma_R > \sigma_{T0}) $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 1
$$ \eta_2 = (\mu_T-\mu_R)^2 + (\sigma_T^2-\sigma_R^2) - \theta_p\,\sigma_{T0}^2 \quad (\sigma_R \le \sigma_{T0}) $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 1
$$ \theta_p = \frac{(\ln 1.11)^2 + 0.01}{0.1^2} = 2.0891 $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 1

Mean squares (Step 2); $\bar X_{ijk\cdot}$ is the mean of the m stages of a unit, $\bar X_{\cdot\cdot k\cdot}$ the product mean:

$$ MSB_k = \frac{m \sum_{j=1}^{\ell_k}\sum_{i=1}^{n_k} (\bar X_{ijk\cdot} - \bar X_{\cdot\cdot k\cdot})^2}{n_k \ell_k - 1} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 2
$$ MSW_k = \frac{\sum_{j}\sum_{i}\sum_{s=1}^{m} (X_{ijks} - \bar X_{ijk\cdot})^2}{n_k \ell_k (m-1)} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 2
$$ \hat\sigma_k = \sqrt{\frac{MSB_k}{m} + \frac{(m-1)\,MSW_k}{m}}, \qquad \hat\Delta = \bar X_{\cdot\cdot T\cdot} - \bar X_{\cdot\cdot R\cdot} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Steps 1 and 3

Linearized point estimate and 95% upper confidence bound (Step 4; α = 0.05). For each component the point estimate $E_q$, the confidence bound $H_q$ and $U_q = (H_q - E_q)^2$:

$$ E_D = \hat\Delta^2, \qquad H_D = \left(|\hat\Delta| + t_{1-\alpha,\; n_T\ell_T + n_R\ell_R - 2}\sqrt{\frac{MSB_T}{n_T\ell_T m} + \frac{MSB_R}{n_R\ell_R m}}\right)^2 $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 4
$$ E_1 = \frac{MSB_T}{m}, \qquad H_1 = \frac{(\ell_T n_T - 1)\,E_1}{\chi^2_{\ell_T n_T - 1,\;\alpha}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 4
$$ E_2 = \frac{(m-1)\,MSW_T}{m}, \qquad H_2 = \frac{\ell_T n_T (m-1)\,E_2}{\chi^2_{\ell_T n_T (m-1),\;\alpha}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 4
$$ E_{3s} = -(1+\theta_p)\frac{MSB_R}{m}, \qquad H_{3s} = \frac{(\ell_R n_R - 1)\,E_{3s}}{\chi^2_{\ell_R n_R - 1,\;1-\alpha}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 4
$$ E_{4s} = -(1+\theta_p)\frac{(m-1)\,MSW_R}{m}, \qquad H_{4s} = \frac{\ell_R n_R (m-1)\,E_{4s}}{\chi^2_{\ell_R n_R (m-1),\;1-\alpha}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 4
$$ H_{\eta 1} = (E_D + E_1 + E_2 + E_{3s} + E_{4s}) + \sqrt{U_D + U_1 + U_2 + U_{3s} + U_{4s}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 4; Hyslop et al. (2000) §3
$$ H_{\eta 2} = (E_D + E_1 + E_2 + E_{3c} + E_{4c} - \theta_p\sigma_{T0}^2) + \sqrt{U_D + U_1 + U_2 + U_{3c} + U_{4c}} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 4

In the constant-scaled criterion $E_{3c} = -MSB_R/m$ and $E_{4c} = -(m-1)MSW_R/m$ (H and U terms with the same degrees of freedom). $\chi^2_{\nu,\alpha}$ is the α quantile of the chi-square distribution with ν degrees of freedom: $\Pr(\chi^2_\nu \le \chi^2_{\nu,\alpha}) = \alpha$. If m = 1, $E_2$, $E_4$ and their H and U terms are left out. The structure of the bound is the extension by Hyslop et al. (2000) of Howe's (1974) approximation.

Modified one-sided PBE (only if the product guidance calls for it). If the test mean is below the reference mean, the mean difference is left out of the criterion:

$$ \frac{(\sigma_T^2-\sigma_R^2)}{\sigma_R^2} \le \theta_p \quad (\mu_T < \mu_R): \qquad E_D = H_D = U_D = 0 $$ :: FDA PSG 020121 (fluticasone propionate nasal spray, December 2025) appendix, Steps 1–3

## 4. Decision rule
$$ H_\eta \le 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{PBE established} $$ :: PSG 020929 Appendix A, Step 5; FDA (2026) Statistical Approaches to Establishing Bioequivalence

$H_\eta$ is the reference-scaled bound ($H_{\eta 1}$) if the estimated $\sigma_R > 0.1$, otherwise the constant-scaled bound ($H_{\eta 2}$) (Step 3).

## 5. Implementation in the platform
- Core: `calc_pbe` (`pbe_core.R`, pinned with SHA-256; FSR-VAL-PBE-1 v1.1: 213 checks against an independent Python reference). The bridge `pbe_kopru.R` checks the input (25 checks): repeated measurements at the same container and stage, missing stages and different stage sets are rejected.
- The screen reads the CSV with `platform_csv`; the computation is passed to the bridge unchanged.
- The decision is $H_\eta \le 0$; equality with 0 also means “established”.
- In an unbalanced but complete design (different numbers of units per batch) the formulas are applied over all units and a warning is given; the PSG examples are balanced.

## 7. Limits
- The PSG recommends at least 3 batches and at least 10 units per batch; smaller designs are computed with a warning.
- The batch variance is not estimated separately; the properties of the PSG estimator under large batch-to-batch variability are studied in FSR-TN-PBE-01.
- One measured quantity per analysis; several quantities (e.g. DD and ISM) are analysed separately.
- For research and education; not a system validated for GxP environments.
